Why these exist

  • No dependencies at all. Only what Node 22 ships with, so there is no supply chain to trust and nothing to install.
  • Checked against the chain. Free APIs get things wrong: RugCheck can list closed accounts and stale pool shares, so holders are re-verified on-chain.
  • Read-only. No tool asks for a private key or signs anything. The paper trader only spends fake SOL.
